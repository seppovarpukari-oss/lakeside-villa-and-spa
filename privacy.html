Privacy

Privacy at Lakeside Villa & Spa

This notice explains how information is handled when you use the Lakeside Villa & Spa website and Villa Assistant.

Data controller

The data controller is Lakeside Villa & Spa. Privacy enquiries can be sent to info@lakesidevillaandspa.com.

Villa Assistant conversations

When you use Villa Assistant, your question and the assistant's answer may be stored together with limited technical context such as language, page, referrer and campaign parameters. The purpose is to operate, improve, secure and review the assistant.

Conversation records are kept for no longer than 12 calendar months and are then automatically removed.

Voice dictation and AI processing

If you use dictation, the recording is sent to OpenAI for transcription only after you press Done. Cancelling removes the recording without sending it. Villa Assistant also uses AI services to generate responses. Service providers may process the submitted content only as needed to provide these functions under their applicable data-processing terms.

Analytics

With the choices you make in the site's cookie preferences, aggregate usage information may be processed through analytics services. Conversation text is not sent to Google Analytics. Assistant analytics are limited to aggregate events such as opening the assistant, asking a question or encountering an error.

What we do not intentionally store

The Villa Assistant conversation store does not intentionally create a persistent visitor profile and does not intentionally store IP addresses as part of the conversation record.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal data, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority. Requests can be sent to info@lakesidevillaandspa.com.
