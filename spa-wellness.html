Private Pool

Morning water at the heart of the villa

The pool changes with the day. At sunrise, soft eastern light reaches the water and turns the spa into a calm private morning ritual.

Hydrotherapy

Water massage, built into the pool

Two dedicated water-massage stations are integrated into the private pool — one below the surface and one above the waterline for a different kind of pressure and relaxation.

Finnish Sauna

Heat, wood and silence

A full-size Finnish sauna in warm timber, designed for slow heat rather than spectacle. It is the natural counterpart to the pool: swim, warm up, cool down, repeat.

Steam Sauna

A softer kind of heat

The steam sauna adds a different pace to the wellness circuit — humid, quiet and restorative. Its warm mosaic surfaces create a more intimate atmosphere beside the pool and Finnish sauna.

Fireside Room

Between sauna and pool

A private fireside room connects the wellness spaces and gives the evening somewhere to slow down. Sit by the fireplace between sauna rounds, or simply stay a little longer before returning upstairs.
